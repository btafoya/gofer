  kubectl logs deploy/{{ include "gofer.fullname" . }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} | grep setup_token

Operational notes:
- Gofer runs exactly one replica on a single SQLite volume. Upgrades stop the pod before starting the new one (strategy Recreate).
- Back up the data volume (gofer.db, secret.key, VAPID keys, accounts/) before upgrades.
- Losing GOFER_SECRET_KEY makes stored credentials unreadable. Keep it in your secret manager.
- Run `helm test {{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}` to check the service answers.
